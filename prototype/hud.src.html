What Each Panel Does

reference for the layout above
1

Target readout

Nameplate, hull %, range, and which broadside arc the target sits in. Only shown while an enemy is in view.

2

Wind indicator

Relative wind direction and its speed effect — the main input to every positioning decision, so it sits top-center where the eye already is.

3

Minimap

Heading, nearby ships, and coastline at a glance. Always on, top-right per genre convention.

4

Hull & sail status

Your two damage tracks, always visible bottom-left — hull is the health bar, sail governs speed and turn rate.

5

Broadside control

Port/starboard reload state and active ammo type — the primary combat input, anchored bottom-center directly under the ship.

Open

The panels are deliberately dark glass so they hold up over a bright sky. Worth deciding whether they should fade out when nothing is happening, leaving only the wind indicator and broadside pips on screen during ordinary sailing.
