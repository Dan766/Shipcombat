Ship Classes

Four hulls on a single axis: speed and turn traded for hull and broadside weight. No matchup counters — a heavier ship wins any trade it can force, and a lighter ship's whole job is refusing that trade. They are told apart by rig, not by scale: a fore-and-aft sloop, two square-riggers of different height, and a lateen-mizzened galleon. Every render is built from the same class definitions the game would use.

Still to tune

The 1–5 ratings are placed evenly to state intent, not because they are balanced. The real question is whether the Sloop's turn advantage is actually enough to survive a Galleon's broadside at close range — that needs playtesting, not a table. Class-specific abilities are deliberately out of scope for now; see GAME_DESIGN.md.
