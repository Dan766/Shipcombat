Construction Rules

what makes it read as this game and not generic low-poly

Ambient dominates the sun

Fill light carries roughly twice the key. Faces turned away from the sun stay bright and colorful — nothing crushes to black. This is what makes it feel airy rather than dramatic.

Distance haze does the depth

Everything fades toward the horizon color with distance. It replaces detail, sells scale, and lets far geometry be extremely cheap.

Flat facets, no gradients

One flat fill per triangle. All shape reads from the angle between neighboring facets, never from blended shading or texture.

Faceted water, damped by distance

Waves are real displaced geometry — choppy near the camera, flattening toward the horizon so the far sea reads calm instead of noisy.

Chunky silhouettes

A ship is a hull, a mast, and two or three sails. If it isn't recognizable as a silhouette at minimap size, it has too many parts.

Cut at the waterline

Hulls are clipped where they meet the sea, so ships sit in the water rather than on it — the single detail that most sells the ocean.
