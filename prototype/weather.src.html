Ship Combat — Conditions

Weather

Five sea states, from glassy to gale. Every shot below is the same brig, the same camera and the same seed — only the weather changes, so any difference you can see is the weather and nothing else. Wave height drives gunnery, wind drives speed, and visibility decides how late you see anyone coming.

Undecided: whether weather is fixed for a match or shifts during one. Fixed is simpler to balance and lets players pick a ship against known conditions; shifting is more dramatic and makes the Sloop's early lead genuinely fragile. A middle option is to fix the state at match start but telegraph one change — a squall rolling in on a timer everyone can see. Tracked in GAME_DESIGN.md.

Conditions v0.1 — sky, light rig, fog range, sea state and rain are all preset data (prototype/weather.js). Foam thresholds are set from the measured wave-height distribution, so whitecaps cover a comparable share of the surface as the sea builds.
